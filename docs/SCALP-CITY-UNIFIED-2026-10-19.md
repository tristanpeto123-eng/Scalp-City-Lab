## C. Canonical end-to-end lifecycle and authority
1. **Acquire:** completed, provenance-tagged, validated GET-only historical market bars; reject stale, malformed, discontinuous or forward-leaking candles.
2. **Research:** baseline, invented-rule, diversified V2, or optional DQN. Give each model a chart-scoped version, data digest, parameters/weights and run lineage; preserve disjoint chronological splits and a sealed holdout. Candidate generation alone is not teaching evidence.
3. **Screen:** fee/slippage, maximum drawdown, observed trade count, sensitivity, benchmark and train/validation gap. Checker performs deterministic negative/leakage tests and compares alternative lanes without mixing their experimental evidence.
4. **Freeze and audit:** candidate SHA and sources become immutable; independent Teacher reassesses untouched holdout data and rejects simulator/auditor mismatches. No auto-enrolment from an invented composite rule or neural policy until a separately approved compatible auditor exists.
5. **Examine:** only independently eligible candidates may request one of the three *separate* PAPER exam seats under an explicitly released and configured examiner. Signed, broker-reconciled forward trades and fees establish official grades; required forward evidence includes at least 30 closed trades. Preflight does not mean orders have run.
6. **Present:** Academy exposes provisional historical lessons separately from forward verified results. Trading Floor offers DEMO and locked PAPER controls; real-money controls require exact owner approval, independent risk controls and full end-to-end commissioning.
7. **Audit:** immutable evidence and explicit no-claim-without-proof status returned to Mission Control. Brain/Checker may flag stale work but cannot silently trade or approve a release.
