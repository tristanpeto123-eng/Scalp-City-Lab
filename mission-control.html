WHY THESE GATES MATTER

Results have different meanings

Development result: a repeatable historical observation. It can point to models worth examining; it cannot prove predictive success.

Historical qualification: an independent examination of frozen parameters against later, previously untouched data.

Verified PAPER grade: reconciled fills, fees, drawdown and at least 30 closed forward trades after admission.
