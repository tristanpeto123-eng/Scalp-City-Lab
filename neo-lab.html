Meet NEO

One separate pattern-memory research agent for ten authentic five-minute crypto charts. NEO retains timestamped, normalised OHLCV pattern signatures and searches similar earlier examples. It is not a visual neural network that remembers every pixel.

Learning and graduation

NEO's past-labelled patterns are restricted to the original training boundary. Previously reserved validation or sealed holdout candles cannot silently become training labels when the source window moves.

Each pupil continues learning from its research checkpoint. A candidate can be archived as an immutable version for external paper evaluation; the learner remains free to improve. NEO cannot grade pupils, authorize trading-floor promotions or send orders.
