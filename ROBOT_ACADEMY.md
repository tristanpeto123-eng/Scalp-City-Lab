# Scalp City Robot Academy — approved UI specification

## Purpose
One robot pupil per crypto pair, a colourful school metaphor for **observed winning-trade percentage**. Grades 0 through 9 represent 0–9.999…%, 10–19.999…%, etc. Grade 10 is exactly 100%. **Orientation** means insufficient or missing trade evidence.

## Evidence requirements
- The initial published academy has **zero independently verified forward-paper trade records** and must show every robot ungraded.
- Clicking **Explore simulated classroom** shows clearly labelled *fictional* 100-trade demonstration results. Clicking **Simulate a training lesson** changes those demo fixtures only. **Show actual status** resets the UI to ungraded.
- A grade is provisional unless the record is independently validated and has at least **30 unique closed forward-paper trades**. A sample of 30 is a UI minimum, not proof of profitable strategy.
- For each pupil the report card separates grade, observed win rate, **realized net GBP profit**, **max drawdown**, champion strategy/model, and evidence status. 80% winners can still lose money after fees or through large losses.
- Data from historical backtests, training sets, replayed windows, generated tests, screenshots or user-authored JSON **must never** populate a *verified* grade.
- Future trusted data ingestion should authenticate backend, validate broker forward-paper trade IDs and order/fill reconciliation, de-duplicate unique closes, compute net GBP correctly (conversion and fees), and bind each result to exactly one pair and one selected model. JSON's self-reported source field is not authentication.
- Champion strategies are input hypotheses for future training, not automatic permission to place orders, graduate, or approve release. Final broker actions remain blocked.

## Integration contract
`robot-academy-schema.json` specifies a planned **server-issued** forward-paper feed. Required: source, unique trades, profit/loss, drawdown, champion model and provenance. The current page does **not** fetch or trust the forward-paper feed; it only loads a separate historical-training summary that is explicitly unverified. `window.RobotAcademy.previewVerifiedRecord()` performs shape checks for a *client-side preview only* and never changes official grades.

## GitHub release
Static UI: `robot-academy.html`. Navigation from `strategy-validation.html`. Node checks: `robot-academy-tests.mjs` via `.github/workflows/robot-academy-tests.yml`.

Visual school grades are educational; not investment returns, approval for real accounts, or a brokerage platform.

## Milestone 2 — real training-history integration (October 2026)

The Academy now loads `research-data/robot-academy.json`, generated by `scripts/export-robot-academy.mjs` **from the separate GitHub `scalp-learning-memory` branch**. This is a distinct historical TRAINING feed, not the future forward-paper feed described above.

- The 10 pupils use their own symbol's saved experimentation history. Each `generation` is a saved, fingerprinted experiment, **not** a newly observed market sample or an independent trading result.
- The visible provisional classroom grade is calculated from the **reported rounded historical training winRatePct** for the per-pair best available strategy (ranked by training net GBP over a minimum of 20 historical closes). Exact winning-trade counts cannot be recovered reliably from a percentage rounded to two decimal places; the exporter does not fabricate that count.
- A *research leader* is the best available training-only parameter candidate; it remains separate from a **validated champion**. A loss-making research leader is explicitly labelled LOSS-MAKING. An observed high win rate can coincide with net losses.
- For each pair the report card shows training closes, historical GBP-equivalent net return of the selected candidate, its experimental strategy, trial count, independent training-window count, latest training date and lesson-by-lesson provisional grade history.
- A saved second experiment on the same historical candles is useful **parameter-search memory**, but never an additional independent validation period. Real newer price data and independent forward-paper results are required to prove improvement.
- `.github/workflows/robot-academy-sync.yml` reads the memory branch with GitHub-held permissions and publishes a **deterministic, sanitised static summary**, every six hours and via manual dispatch. This job does **not** train models; it syncs the results of training that actually occurred elsewhere. With unchanged saved history, the summary does not change.
- The published browser fetch is read-only and validates source labels, numeric constraints, market symbols and the absence of any claimed verified-grade data; malformed/unavailable JSON results in **ungraded** pupils, never invented results.
- The independent `robot-academy-schema.json` still describes a future authenticated forward-paper data service. Static JSON, even if stored on GitHub, is **not proof of executed brokerage trades**.
- Demo lessons never modify the stored research history. No active external model training has been scheduled by this integration; that requires an independently approved learning-run workflow and valid broker-data access.

**Safety gate:** Do not enable broker orders, deposits, withdrawal, automatic model promotion or call historical training P&L genuine forward performance. Win-rate grades are an educational display, not an investable performance metric.
